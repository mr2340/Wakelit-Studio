# Wakelit Story Quality Checklist (QC Scorecard)
*The Definitive 100-Point Pass/Fail Production Gate*

---

## Instructions

Before any script or outline is passed to Google Flow prompt synthesis or video editing, it must be rigorously evaluated against this 100-point rubric. 

- **Minimum Passing Score:** **90 / 100 points**.
- **Automatic Disqualification:** Any failure in the "Instant Reject Conditions" terminates the review immediately; the script must be rewritten from Stage 3.

---

## 1. Instant Reject Conditions (Zero-Tolerance)

If ANY of the following 5 conditions are present, the script fails automatically:

- [ ] **Lethal Condition 1:** The script contains direct lecturing, moralizing, or instructional commands ("You should", "Make sure you", "Here is a tip").
- [ ] **Lethal Condition 2:** Ayo has dialogue spoken directly to the camera or breaks the fourth wall.
- [ ] **Lethal Condition 3:** Ayo's physical design deviates from the master reference (must have `#613729` head, `#D2A23E` hoodie, zero ears, zero nose, zero neck, zero fingers).
- [ ] **Lethal Condition 4:** The Shade is depicted as an external alien, physical zombie, or blood-and-gore horror monster rather than an internal shadow-self.
- [ ] **Lethal Condition 5:** The turning point occurs later than 65% of the total duration (video drags or loses retention).

---

## 2. The 100-Point Scoring Rubric

### Category A: Narrative Structure & Retention Architecture (30 Points)
- [ ] **Hook Velocity (10 pts):** Does Scene 1 establish immediate visual curiosity and emotional conflict within 3 seconds? (No slow fade-ins, no logo cards).
- [ ] **Pacing & Micro-Curiosity Gaps (10 pts):** Does every scene transition ask an unanswered question? Does the auditory landscape shift every 30 seconds?
- [ ] **The Void / Climax Placement (10 pts):** Is there an absolute auditory drop (-50dB silence) and structural turning point precisely between 50% and 60% of runtime?

### Category B: Emotional Resonance & Character Vulnerability (25 Points)
- [ ] **Shared Vulnerability (10 pts):** Does the story depict genuine, private human struggle without cliché bravado? Does the viewer see their own private shame reflected?
- [ ] **Earned Transformation (10 pts):** Is the resolution achieved through painful, physical micro-action rather than magical realization or luck?
- [ ] **Quiet Catharsis (5 pts):** Does the ending provide authentic, grounded hope without preaching an unrealistic overnight transformation?

### Category C: Visual Pantomime & Metaphor (25 Points)
- [ ] **Mute Comprehension (10 pts):** Can the full narrative arc and emotional stakes be understood with the audio on complete mute?
- [ ] **Symbolic Prop Transformation (10 pts):** Is there at least one physical prop (shoes, notebook, clock, laptop) whose emotional meaning inverts between Act 1 and Act 3?
- [ ] **Chromatic Conflict (5 pts):** Does the visual progression strictly adhere to the Cold Blue (`#1B2A4A`) to Warm Amber (`#E89D42`) lighting law?

### Category D: Production & Engine Feasibility (20 Points)
- [ ] **Scene Count & Timings (5 pts):** Are there exactly 12 scenes (for 150s film), each between 8 and 14 seconds long?
- [ ] **Flow Prompt Compliance (10 pts):** Are all visual prompts formatted with consistent character tokens, 3D clay/vinyl tactile textures, and 2.39:1 widescreen framing?
- [ ] **Foley & Sound Specification (5 pts):** Are all foley cues mapped to existing CC0 assets (rain, clock tick, phone buzz, footsteps, door)?
