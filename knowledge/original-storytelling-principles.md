# Original Storytelling Principles & Universe Rules
*Preserving Wakelit Identity & Preventing Derivative Homogenization*

---

## 1. Core Commitment: Distinct Creative Identity

While Wakelit studies narrative mechanics from pioneers like Day One Films, Pixar, and philosophical creators, **Wakelit is never a clone, copycat, or derivative imitator**.

We emulate **structural discipline, psychological precision, and emotional truth**—never surface assets, dialogue, or visual signatures.

| Domain | What We Learn & Emulate | What We NEVER Copy (Wakelit Original Law) |
|---|---|---|
| **Character** | The universal relatability of an avatar in conflict. | We NEVER copy Day One's white mannequin, hair styles, or facial masks. Our character is **Ayo**: warm umber face (`#613729`), mustard yellow hoodie (`#D2A23E`), no neck, no ears, no nose, no fingers, stylized 3D vinyl/clay anatomy. |
| **Antagonist** | Externalizing internal demons into a visible entity. | We NEVER copy another creator's monster designs. Our entity is **The Shade**: smoky obsidian silhouettes, soft rim light, pale blue horizontal slit eyes (`#9CC4FF`), and tendrils that merge with screen glow. |
| **Dialogue** | Sparse, poetic, whispered ghost conscience. | We NEVER copy scripts, catchphrases, or lines from other videos. Every word written for Wakelit is original and tailored to Ayo's specific thematic world. |
| **Color Palette** | Cinematic contrast between light and dark. | Our specific chromatic signature: Cold Blue (`#1B2A4A`) vs Warm Amber (`#E89D42`) vs Mustard Ochre (`#D2A23E`). |
| **Brand Tagline** | Compelling thematic anchor. | Wakelit's official, trademarked identity: **"Every night ends."** |

## 2. Wakelit Universe Rules (The Lore Bible)

### 2.1 Character Identity: Ayo
- **Species/Form:** Stylized minimalist human avatar.
- **Color Codes:** Head/Face skin tone `#613729`, Mustard Ochre Hoodie `#D2A23E`, Dark Charcoal Sweatpants `#222222`.
- **Anatomy Rules:** No nose, no ears, no visible neck, no separate fingers (stylized mitten hands with articulated thumbs). Eyes are expressive dark micro-spheres with clean specular reflections.
- **Personality:** Creative, ambitious, susceptible to fatigue, deeply self-reflective, resilient when pushed to the brink.

### 2.2 The Antagonist: The Shade
- **Nature:** Non-biological. It is an astral manifestation of inertia, digital sedation, and avoidance.
- **Behavior:** Seductive before aggressive. It does not slash or roar; it wraps, calms, numbs, and traps. It offers the sweet poison of "later".
- **Physical Traits:** Smoky obsidian geometry, soft luminous blue edge rim, two narrow horizontal slit eyes (`#9CC4FF`). When defeated, it does not die in a gory explosion; it evaporates like morning mist or shrinks into a harmless speck.

### 2.3 The Physical Environment: The Crucible Room
- A minimalist bedroom/studio with hardwood floorboards, a plain wooden desk, a simple bed, and a large rain-streaked window with vertical blinds.
- The room expands or contracts visually depending on Ayo's psychological state:
  - *In defeat:* The walls seem 10 feet closer, shadows swallow the corners, ceiling lowers.
  - *In victory:* The room feels vast, breathable, bathed in horizontal shafts of golden dawn light.

## 3. Creative Development Standards

1. **Originality Guarantee:** Every script concept must be checked against known YouTube videos. If an idea feels too close to an existing video, it must be radically re-angled or discarded.
2. **Subtle Cultural Soul:** Ayo draws subtle design influence from global contemporary youth culture—clean streetwear aesthetics, urban solitude, and universal modern digital living.
3. **Longevity Over Trend-Chasing:** Wakelit does not make videos about fleeting internet memes, crypto crashes, or ephemeral trends. We address eternal human struggles that will be as relevant in 2050 as they are today.
