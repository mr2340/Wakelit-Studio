# Creative Direction Guidelines

> **Purpose:** Living guide defining creative tone, storytelling principles, pacing, and visual conventions for GHD Automated Studio productions.
> **Status:** Initialized. Awaiting progressive user training and creative examples.

---

## 1. Core Production Philosophy
- **Message-First Visual Storytelling:** Visuals exist to serve, amplify, and elevate the voiceover and emotional narrative, not merely illustrate words literally.
- **Intentional Pacing:** Scene durations are dictated by emotional weight, rhythm, and narrative purpose, not arbitrary equal time divisions.
- **Minimalist Character World:** The character universe is cohesive, stripped of visual noise, and emotionally resonant.

---

## 2. Emotional Tone & Mood Matrix
*(To be populated as creative direction is taught)*
- **Contemplative / Reflective:** Slower camera drift, negative space, controlled lighting.
- **Vulnerable / Isolated:** Characters framed small in wide or muted environments; deliberate silence.
- **Tension / Transition:** Subtle camera push, lighting shifts, shifts in depth of field.
- **Resolution / Hope:** Warmer ambient light, open horizons, gentle head/posture lift.

---

## 3. Negative Directives (What We NEVER Do)
- Never create generic, chaotic stock-style AI video collages.
- Never let characters suddenly change fundamental anatomy, facial geometry, or art style between cuts.
- Never use rapid, disorienting cuts that disrupt voiceover comprehension.
- Never invent unrelated background lore or unapproved accessories without explicit brief requirements.
