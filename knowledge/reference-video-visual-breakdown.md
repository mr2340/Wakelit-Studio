## 3. Comparative Synthesis: What Wakelit Adopts vs. Rejects

### What Wakelit Adopts `[OUR CHOICE]`
1. **The Letterboxed Side-On Theatrical Stage:** 2.39:1 active picture (y 96–984 @ 1080p).
2. **The Chromatic Law:** Cold Blue (temptation/avoidance) vs Warm Amber (progress/agency).
3. **The Dead Silence Device:** 0.0dB audio cut immediately preceding decisive turning points.
4. **The Time-Skip Black Card:** 2.63-second black card with centered 66px text.
5. **The Eyebrow-Driven Emotion:** Facial expression restricted entirely to two curved brows and tiny white eye catchlights.

### What Wakelit Strictly Rejects / Forbids `[ORIGINALITY]`
1. **No Plagiarism of Character Assets:** Ayo has distinct proportions, deep brown `#613729` head, mustard ochre `#D2A23E` hoodie, and specific mitten thumb bump. Never clone Day One Films' cream-colored lead.
2. **No Preaching or Moralizing:** Wakelit films never lecture (*"You must quit lust today"*). Our characters act and experience consequences; the audience draws their own conclusions.
3. **No 3D CGI Shading:** Strictly flat 2D vector/cartoon illustration. No shiny plastic or Octane render textures.
