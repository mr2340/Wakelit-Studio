# Character Guidelines (Ayo & The Cast)

> **Purpose:** Master rulebook for character anatomy, proportions, approved expressions, and cast design.
> **Source:** `Character_Ayo_looking_tired_and_5ce88056_2K.jpg` and `AGENT_BRIEF_01`.

---

## 1. Ayo: Master Anatomy & Construction
Ayo is a stylized, flat 2D **"bean in a hoodie"**. He is deliberately non-realistic and carries maximum emotional weight through minimalism.

### Six Fundamental Shapes:
1. **The Body:** Soft pill/capsule shape in mustard hoodie (`#D2A23E`). Slightly wider than the head.
2. **The Head:** Smooth round ball in deep brown (`#613729`), merged directly into the hoodie. **NO neck.**
3. **The Hood:** Soft collar-like fold of mustard cloth around the base of the head. **Always down, never over his head.**
4. **The Arms:** Noodle arms in mustard sleeves ending in **mitten hands** (round blob with tiny thumb bump). **NO individual fingers.**
5. **The Legs:** Short, thin grey legs (`#666668`) extending from under the hoodie.
6. **The Shoes:** Small rounded white blobs (`#EDEEF0`).

### Proportions:
- **Head Height:** Exactly **1/3** of total standing height.
- **Hoodie Width:** **1.25x** head width.
- **Leg Length:** Short, approximately **1/5** of total height.
- **Arm Length:** Hands hang level with the bottom hem of the hoodie.

## 2. Facial Features & The Seven Approved Expressions
The face contains **only three elements**:
1. **Eyes:** Two solid black round dots with a tiny white pin-prick highlight in each.
2. **Eyebrows:** Short, curved expressive strokes. **Brows convey 90% of the emotion.**
3. **Mouth:** Small subtle line or gentle curve.
*No nose. No ears. No cheeks. No nostrils. No hair.*

| Expression | Brows | Eyes | Mouth | Narrative Beat |
|------------|-------|------|-------|----------------|
| **Tired / Worried** | Inner ends raised, sloping down outwards | Half-lidded (flat cut across top of dots) | Small flat line | Nighttime loop, fatigue, guilt |
| **Blank / Neutral** | Straight and horizontal | Round, open dots | Tiny straight line | Waiting, observing, numb |
| **Determined** | Lowered, angled inward in a sharp "V" | Focused, round dots | Small firm horizontal line | The turning point, running |
| **Afraid / Shocked** | Raised high on forehead | Enlarged dots (~130% size) | Small open "o" | The critical realization |
| **Relieved / Soft Smile** | Gentle relaxed arch | Soft, slightly lidded | Small upward curve | Morning, breakthrough, recovery |
| **Sleeping** | None visible | Closed, two thin arcs | Small relaxed line | Safe resting, healed nights |
| **Sad / Low** | Inner ends strongly peaked | Large watery highlight | Small downward curve | Regret, loneliness |

## 3. The 10-Point "Ayo Test" (QC Gate)
Before approving any generated shot containing Ayo, every question must be YES:
1. Is the head deep brown (`#613729`) and round?
2. Is the hoodie mustard (`#D2A23E`) with the hood down?
3. Are there two distinct black dot eyes with white highlights?
4. Do the eyebrows strictly match one of the 7 approved expressions?
5. Is the face free of any nose, ears, cheeks, or neck?
6. Are the hands solid mittens without individual fingers?
7. Are the trousers grey (`#666668`) and shoes white (`#EDEEF0`)?
8. Is the outline thin, dark, and slightly hand-drawn (~2px)?
9. Is the entire character within the active letterbox area (y 96–984)?
10. Is the coloring completely flat with zero 3D gradients or realistic shading?

## 4. The Cast: Family-Resemblance Rules
All supporting characters are built from the exact same recipe:
- Same bean body, round neckless head, mitten hands, dot eyes, and thin outline.
- Differentiated by: **Head color**, **hair/hat silhouette**, **clothing color**, and **one simple accessory**.
- **Ayo is the ONLY character in mustard/amber.** No other character may wear amber.
- Supporting cast:
  - **Tobi (Best friend):** Head `#94603F`, white backwards cap, teal tee (`#3E8E8A`).
  - **Emeka (Quiet friend):** Head `#3B2418`, short low-cut hair, brick-red tee (`#B5523B`), small round glasses.
  - **Amaka (Kind classmate):** Head `#7A4A32`, dark afro puff, soft-pink hoodie (`#D98E8E`), single hair clip.
  - **Mama:** Head `#613729`, green head-wrap (`#3E7A52`), purple blouse (`#7A5AA8`).
  - **Mr. Bello (Teacher/Coach):** Head `#3B2418`, grey cardigan (`#8A8F99`), whistle on neck cord.

## 5. The Antagonist: The Shade
- Soft, solid-black ink blob (`#000000`).
- Two glowing slit eyes in pale blue (`#9CC4FF`). Eyes curve into crescents when smiling. No mouth.
- Subtle cold-blue rim light glow (~3px).
- Scales dynamically according to story stage (from eyes peeking under the bed up to the ceiling-high "Beast"). Never physically touches Ayo.
