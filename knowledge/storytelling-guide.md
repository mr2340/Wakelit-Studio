# Storytelling Guide

> **Purpose:** Principles of narrative pacing, voiceover-visual synchronization, and visual beat breakdown.
> **Status:** Initialized. Configured for non-linear, intelligent pacing.

---

## 1. Non-Linear Pacing Principles
- **No Arbitrary Slicing:** A 2-minute video is NEVER simply sliced into twelve identical 10-second blocks.
- **Narrative Arc Mapping:**
  - **Hook / Opening Beat:** Establish tone and context. Can be lingering and deliberate.
  - **Inciting Emotion / Rising Action:** Shorter, focused cuts or continuous gradual movement.
  - **Climactic Realization:** High emotional intensity; often calls for visual stillness or high-contrast framing.
  - **Resolution / Lingering Echo:** Open framing, spacious pauses.

---

## 2. Intentional Silence & Black Screen Moments
- **Black Screens (`black_screen`):** Used deliberately for dramatic emphasis, auditory absorption of pivotal lines, or sensory resets between major thematic shifts.
- **Visual Stillness:** A static or near-static visual allows the audience to deeply absorb spoken voiceover without distraction.
- **Voiceover Across Cuts:** A single continuous voiceover thought can extend across multiple scene perspectives.

---

## 3. Scene Breakdown Decision Matrix
When planning a scene from a brief and voiceover segment:
1. What is the emotional core of this sentence?
2. Does this beat require character presence, environmental focus, or pure black screen?
3. Should the camera be stationary (intimacy/calm) or moving (passage of time/emotional shift)?
4. What is the minimum and maximum duration needed for this idea to breathe?
