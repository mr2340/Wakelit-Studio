# Visual Style Guidelines (Wakelit)

> **Purpose:** Defines the exact aesthetic, lighting, palette, framing, and environment rules for all Wakelit productions.
> **Source:** Synthesized from `AGENT_BRIEF_01` and measured reference video analysis (`Day One Films`).

---

## 1. Screen Format & Geometry
- **Master Resolution:** 1920 × 1080 @ 30 fps.
- **Letterbox Bars:** Top and bottom black bars of **96 px** each.
- **Active Picture Area:** **1920 × 888**, stretching from **y = 96 to y = 984** (aspect ratio ~2.16:1).
- **Safe Composition Rule:** Every character, prop, and caption must remain inside the active picture. The outer 96 px top and bottom are masked.
- **Thumbnails Exception:** Thumbnails use the full 16:9 canvas (no letterbox bars).

---

## 2. Stage Construction & Perspective
- **Flat 2D Theatrical Stage:** Side-on stage perspective without complex 3D vanishing points.
- **Floor Line:** Characters always stand or sit on a horizontal plane located **70%–75% down the frame** (y ≈ 750–800).
- **Furniture & Props:** Minimalist geometric silhouettes with thin dark outlines and flat fill colors.

---

## 3. Official Wakelit Color System
The world is predominantly dark, punctuated by small, emotionally meaningful light pools.

| Name | Hex Code | Role & Narrative Meaning |
|------|----------|--------------------------|
| **Ink** | `#0B0D14` | Master night background, deep darkness |
| **Slate** | `#1B2233` | Walls, floors, background furniture |
| **Screen-Blue** | `#6FA8FF` | Laptop/phone glow, temptation, cold isolation |
| **Pale-Blue** | `#9CC4FF` | The Shade's eyes and whisper captions |
| **Wake-Amber** | `#E8A33D` | Doors, sunrise, progress, human warmth, friends |
| **Bone** | `#EDE6D6` | Main caption text, paper notebook |
| **Shade-Red** | `#D6453D` | Bedside digital clock digits ONLY |
| **Ayo Head** | `#613729` | Ayo's skin tone (flat brown) |
| **Ayo Hoodie** | `#D2A23E` | Ayo's signature mustard hoodie & mitten hands |
| **Ayo Legs** | `#666668` | Grey trousers |
| **Ayo Shoes** | `#EDEEF0` | Near-white shoes |

### The Core Color Law:
- **Cold Blue = Temptation / Addiction / The Shade.**
- **Warm Amber = People / Awakening / Freedom / Home.**
- Never saturate an object with both simultaneously. They meet only at the story's climax (the open door at dawn).

---

## 4. Lighting, Atmospheric & Post-Processing Effects
- **Vignette:** 20% to 30% darkening towards the corners of the active picture.
- **Bloom / Soft Glow:** Applied strictly to light sources (lamp, laptop screen, bedside clock, open doorway).
- **Film Grain:** Subtle organic 4% film grain across the composite.
- **Dark Shadow Floor:** Pure deep blacks (`#070608`); never lift shadows into milky gray.

---

## 5. Master Sets

### The Bedroom (The Home Set):
Side-on view, arranged consistently from left to right:
1. **Desk:** Laptop with glowing screen, notebook with dark ink, small desk lamp, wall calendar and analog clock above.
2. **Bed:** Simple dark slate bed, head on left, pillows, window above showing night sky.
3. **Bedside Table:** Digital clock glowing red (`#D6453D`).
4. **Doorway:** Closed door with thin amber light line underneath (`#E8A33D`). Guitar and white running shoes resting beside the door.
