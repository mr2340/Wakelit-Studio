# CREATIVE BRIEF: YOU SAID TOMORROW. YESTERDAY.
*Wakelit Production Studio — Project Manifest #002*

---

## 1. Project Metadata
- **Project Title:** `You Said Tomorrow. Yesterday.`
- **Working Tagline:** *"Every night ends. But tomorrow never comes."*
- **Target Duration:** 150 seconds (2 minutes 30 seconds). Exactly 12 scenes.
- **Production Mode:** **Mode B (Visceral Pantomime + Sparse Ghost Whisper)**.
  - *Justification:* Visual storytelling with tactile Foley (clock tick, pen scratch, phone drop, laptop snap) and typewriter subtitles creates visceral, universal resonance that transcends language barriers and maximizes international retention. Total spoken words: exactly 14 words delivered as an ethereal ghost whisper.
- **Aspect Ratio:** 2.39:1 Anamorphic Widescreen inside 1920x1080 canvas (y 96–984 active safe area).
- **Core Emotional Tone:** Atmospheric, claustrophobic, psychologically unsparing, quiet, deeply cathartic.

## 3. Visual & Chromatic Architecture
- **Act 1 & 2 (0:00–1:18):** **Cold Blue Dominance (90% / 10%).**
  - Screen glare 6500K (`#1B2A4A`, `#3A5A8C`).
  - Nocturnal rain on window pane. Deep obsidian room shadows.
- **The Climax / Turning Point (1:18–1:28):** **The Void (98% Obsidian / 2% Rim).**
  - Absolute black screen. Zero audio.
- **Act 3 (1:28–2:30):** **Warm Amber Influx (70% / 20% / 10%).**
  - Early morning sunlight 3000K (`#E89D42`, `#D2A23E`).
  - Soft atmospheric dust motes. Room expands.

---

## 4. Key Symbolic Props
1. **The Wall Calendar:** A monthly grid with 14 consecutive days crossed out in pencil with the word *"Tomorrow"*.
2. **The Fountain Pen:** Heavy brass/black pen with wet ink nib. Starts hovering; ends writing.
3. **The Blank Notebook:** Raw cream paper with crisp red margin line.
4. **The Smartphone:** Glowing 6500K blue rectangle. The digital pacifier.
5. **The Wall Clock:** High-contrast white circular clock ticking at 60 BPM.
