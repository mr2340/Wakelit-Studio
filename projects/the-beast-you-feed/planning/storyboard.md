# WAKELIT VISUAL STORYBOARD DECK: THE BEAST YOU FEED EVERY NIGHT

**Logline:** At 1:07 AM, a paralyzed young man repeatedly chooses comfort over his dormant ambitions, fueling an obsidian creature of avoidance until silence forces a choice.

**Format:** 2.39:1 Anamorphic (Letterboxed 1920x1080, 96px top/bottom mattes) | **Duration:** 150s (12 Beats)

- **Character Action:** Ayo sits frozen, staring unblinkingly at the blue screen. Minimal 2% chest breathing. Fingers motionless on keyboard.
