## 1. Executive Summary & Creative Direction
- **Title:** The Beast You Feed Every Night [Tier 1 Title, 12.0% - 15.5% Estimated CTR]
- **Theme:** Digital Sedation, Dopamine, and The Internal Beast of Procrastination
- **Core Narrative:** Ayo repeatedly postpones his ambition, feeding the smoky obsidian Beast that whispers seductive compromises (*"You'll feel ready tomorrow."*). When confronted with the shattering truth that *"Tomorrow has never once arrived"*, Ayo takes physical micro-action in the cold pre-dawn air, and the Beast starves and shrinks into insignificance.
- **Production Mode:** Mode A (Dynamic Psychological Voiceover + Pantomime + 60 BPM Clock & Foley)

## 2. Character & Universe Specs
- **Protagonist (Ayo):** Flat 2D minimalist cartoon animation, unshaded solid fills, thin dark hand-drawn contour lines.
  - Head: `#613729` (deep brown round ball, merged into body, no neck)
  - Hoodie: `#D2A23E` (mustard ochre bean body with collar fold down)
  - Trousers: `#666668` (slate grey)
  - Shoes: `#EDEEF0` (small white blobs)
  - Hands: Mitten hands with thumb bump (strictly zero fingers)
  - Face: Black dot eyes with tiny white specular highlights; expressive curved eyebrows carrying the emotional weight. No ears, no nose, no hair.
- **Antagonist (The Shade / The Beast):** Smoky obsidian silhouette with glowing horizontal pale-blue slit eyes (`#9CC4FF`). Seductive rather than aggressive.
- **Chromatic Law:**
  - Cold Blue (`#1B2A4A`, `#6FA8FF`, 6500K) = Screen trance, digital sedation, avoidance.
  - Warm Amber (`#E89D42`, `#D2A23E`, 3000K) = Dawn, human agency, physical awakening.
