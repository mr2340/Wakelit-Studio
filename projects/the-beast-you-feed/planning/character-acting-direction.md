# CHARACTER ACTING DIRECTION: THE BEAST YOU FEED EVERY NIGHT [TESTED]

> **Project:** `projects/the-beast-you-feed`  
> **Character Specs:** Ayo (Protagonist) & The Shade (Antagonist)  
> **Directorial Focus:** Micro-motion, posture shifts, gaze vectors, and locked-camera pantomime across all 12 beats.

---

## Scene-by-Scene Acting & Performance Direction

### Scene 001: Midnight Trance / The Hook (0.0s – 4.0s)
- **Starting Posture:** Ayo seated at wooden desk, spine hunched forward at 20 degrees, elbows resting on desk surface.
- **Head & Gaze:** Head tilted slightly upward, eyes half-lidded, staring fixatedly into the cold blue laptop screen (y=540). Zero eye blinking for the first 2 seconds.
- **Micro-Motion:** At 2.5s, a single slow, shallow chest inhalation. Hood fabric shifts subtly.
- **Limb Action:** Right mitten hand rests motionless on the mouse; left hand lies palm-down limp.
- **Antagonist (The Shade):** Absent or barely perceptible faint obsidian wisp curling along the baseboard behind the chair.

### Scene 002: The Latent Ambition / Dormant Props (4.0s – 8.0s)
- **Starting Posture:** Ayo seated on the floorboards with back against the bed frame, knees pulled halfway up.
- **Head & Gaze:** Head turned down and to the left (45 degrees), looking at the pristine running shoes and untouched sketchbook illuminated in cold moonlight.
- **Micro-Motion:** Head lowers by another 5 degrees; shoulders sag with visible, heavy sorrow.
- **Limb Action:** Left mitten hand reaches out halfway toward the sketchbook, fingers hesitate in mid-air, then withdraw slowly back to his knee.
- **Antagonist (The Shade):** Smoky mist drifts softly across the background carpet, staying low.

### Scene 003: The Whispered Compromise (8.0s – 12.0s)
- **Starting Posture:** Medium profile shot at desk. Ayo leaning in toward the display.
- **Head & Gaze:** Eyes open slightly wider, caught between fatigue and seduction.
- **Micro-Motion:** Ayo's head tilts back slightly as if listening to an internal whisper.
- **Limb Action:** Hand clicks the mouse once (frame 270) with a faint twitch.
- **Antagonist (The Shade):** Smoky obsidian silhouette rises smoothly behind Ayo's chair, draping dark mist over his hoodie shoulders. Pale-blue slit eyes (`#9CC4FF`) glow softly at Ayo's ear level.

### Scene 004: The Sinking Trance (12.0s – 16.0s)
- **Starting Posture:** Over-the-shoulder shot looking down at Ayo's face bathed in flickering cold blue screen reflections.
- **Head & Gaze:** Gaze entirely passive, fixed downward at screen center. Eyes reflect cold digital glow.
- **Micro-Motion:** Mouth parts slightly; facial muscles go completely slack. Breathing slows to near-imperceptible rate.
- **Limb Action:** Hands absent from frame; character is totally immobilized by screen hypnosis.
- **Antagonist (The Shade):** Massive dark tendrils frame the edges of the screen, encroaching inward.

### Scene 005: The Time Jump (2:41 AM) (16.0s – 20.0s)
- **Visual Staging:** Macro close-up of digital clock reading `2:41 AM` in sharp minimalist green LED digits in dark room.
- **Character Action:** In background bokeh (depth of field blur), Ayo's silhouette remains rigidly hunched at desk.
- **Micro-Motion:** Digital digits pulse once; Ayo's head drops another inch in exhaustion.

### Scene 006: The Heavy Collapse (20.0s – 24.0s)
- **Starting Posture:** Ayo standing beside mattress in darkened bedroom.
- **Head & Gaze:** Head hanging low, eyes closed.
- **Micro-Motion:** At 20.5s, Ayo collapses face-down diagonally onto the bed with dead weight.
- **Limb Action:** Arms spread limp across the blanket; hoodie crumples on impact.
- **Antagonist (The Shade):** Large obsidian mass settles directly on top of Ayo's back, dense and oppressive.

### Scene 007: The Void / Sensory Stop (24.0s – 27.0s)
- **Visual Staging:** Pure Black Screen (`#000000`).
- **Character Action:** Total absence of physical form. Mental pause, internal silence.

### Scene 008: The Mirror Confrontation (27.0s – 31.0s)
- **Starting Posture:** Close-up on bathroom mirror. Water droplets clinging to glass surface.
- **Head & Gaze:** Ayo leans over white porcelain sink, splashing cold water. Snaps head up suddenly, staring directly into his own reflection.
- **Micro-Motion:** Eyes snap wide with visceral specular shine. Pupils contract. Heavy, rapid chest breathing.
- **Limb Action:** Both mitten hands press firmly against sink rim, knuckles taut.
- **Antagonist (The Shade):** Towering obsidian form looms behind his mirror reflection, blue slit eyes glowing. Ayo's expression shifts from panic into sharp, unwavering defiance.

### Scene 009: Breaking the Loop / The Snap (31.0s – 35.0s)
- **Starting Posture:** Medium wide shot. Ayo standing upright beside the desk, posture tall and spine erect.
- **Head & Gaze:** Eyes locked firmly on the glowing laptop screen. Jaw set, expression calm and resolute.
- **Micro-Motion:** Shoulders square back; chest expands with deep, steady breath.
- **Limb Action:** Right mitten hand raises deliberately, grips the laptop lid edge, and decisively SLAMS it shut at 32.5s. Blue screen glow vanishes instantly.
- **Antagonist (The Shade):** Hisses, recoils violently away from the desk, scattering into thin tendrils.

### Scene 010: The Physical Stand (35.0s – 39.0s)
- **Starting Posture:** Low-angle shot looking up from floor near the front doorway. Pre-dawn slate-blue sky visible outside.
- **Head & Gaze:** Ayo crouched on one knee, head focused downward on his feet.
- **Micro-Motion:** Rhythmic, confident breathing.
- **Limb Action:** Mitten hands firmly grasp the laces of his clean running shoes, pulling and double-knotting them with precise, decisive motion. Stands up smoothly into full frame.
- **Antagonist (The Shade):** Shrunken into a small, weak puddle on the floorboards, unable to rise.

### Scene 011: Dawn Influx & Dissolution (39.0s – 43.0s)
- **Starting Posture:** Medium shot facing bedroom window.
- **Head & Gaze:** Ayo looks directly at the closed window blinds.
- **Micro-Motion:** Raises hand to the blind wand.
- **Limb Action:** Twists the wand and pulls the cord; blinds part wide. Brilliant warm golden morning sunlight (`#E89D42`) floods the room.
- **Antagonist (The Shade):** Caught directly in the beam of golden light; boils into harmless white steam and dissolves into nothingness.

### Scene 012: Resolution & Awakening (43.0s – 48.0s)
- **Starting Posture:** Wide tracking perspective looking down a sunlit avenue lined with green trees.
- **Head & Gaze:** Ayo running away from camera toward the golden horizon, head held high.
- **Micro-Motion:** Fluid 2D running gait (legs cycling, mustard hoodie bouncing gently, elbows swinging with natural cadence).
- **Limb Action:** Active jogging stride; grounded, purposeful footfalls on pavement.
- **Antagonist (The Shade):** Completely absent. Replaced by open morning sunlight and birds.
