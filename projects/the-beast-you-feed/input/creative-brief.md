# Creative Brief: The Beast You Feed Every Night

> **"Every night ends."**  
> *Official Wakelit Production Creative Brief — Approved Master Story.*

---

## 1. Project Title
**The Beast You Feed Every Night**

## 2. Central Concept & Premise
A visceral, psychologically acute animated short film (200s / 3:20) examining modern procrastination, digital sedation, and the internal monster created by repeatedly choosing tomorrow over today.

Ayo intends to work on his ambition—his running shoes sit dormant by the door, and his blank notebook rests open. Instead, he lingers on his computer screen. Behind him in the shadows, an obsidian smoky creature—**The Shade** (The Beast)—manifests, whispering seductive comforts: *"You'll feel ready tomorrow."*

With every delay, the Beast feeds and grows taller, eventually wrapping around Ayo in suffocating paralysis. At 1:18, an absolute sensory stop occurs—**The Void** (0.0dB Dead Silence). The illusion shatters: *"Tomorrow has never once arrived."* Ayo makes the physical choice to close the laptop, take 5 minutes of action in the cold pre-dawn air, and as the morning amber light breaks across the room, the Beast shrinks to an insignificant wisp.

## 3. Core Universe & Aesthetic Rules
- **Protagonist:** **Ayo** (`#613729` head, `#D2A23E` mustard hoodie, `#666668` trousers, `#EDEEF0` shoes). Flat 2D minimalist cartoon animation, thin wobbly contour outline, no neck, no ears, no nose, no fingers.
- **Antagonist:** **The Shade / The Beast**. Smoky obsidian silhouette with glowing pale-blue horizontal slit eyes (`#9CC4FF`).
- **Chromatic Law:**
  - Cold Blue (`#1B2A4A`, `#6FA8FF`, 6500K) = Screen sedation, digital avoidance, the Beast's domain.
  - Warm Amber (`#E89D42`, `#D2A23E`, 3000K) = Dawn, human agency, awakening, physical action.
- **Audio Law:**
  - 60 BPM mechanical clock tick anchoring the first half.
  - 45 Hz sub-bass drone building tension.
  - 1.1s total dead silence (-50dB drop) at the turning point.
  - Organic CC0 Foley (desk creak, laptop snap, cold footsteps).

## 4. Production Mode
**Mode A / Dynamic Narrative:** Spoken/whispered psychological narration and subtitle layer synchronized with clock rhythm and visual callbacks.

## 5. Narrative Structure (12-Beat Breakdown)
1. **Scene 1 (12s):** Dopamine Hook / Ordinary Dark. Ayo hunched over desk at 1:07 AM, illuminated only by cold blue screen. Clock ticks at 60 BPM.
2. **Scene 2 (13s):** The Latent Ambition. Dormant running shoes by the doorway and blank sketchbook. Finger lingers on mouse.
3. **Scene 3 (13s):** The Whispered Compromise. The Shade emerges from the shadows behind Ayo's chair, whispering: *"You'll feel ready tomorrow."*
4. **Scene 4 (12s):** The Sinking Trance. Blue flicker across Ayo's face; wall clock accelerates.
5. **Scene 5 (15s):** Time-Skip / The Physical Toll. "10 minutes later..." black card. Midnight becomes 2:40 AM. Ayo sinks lower; the Beast expands.
6. **Scene 6 (13s):** The Climax / Approaching The Void. The Beast wraps dark tendrils around Ayo's chest. Screen glare fills Ayo's eyes.
7. **Scene 7 (10s):** The Void (0.0dB Dead Silence). Sudden total sensory drop. Black screen. Whisper: *"Tomorrow has never once arrived."*
8. **Scene 8 (14s):** The Reversal / Physical Decision. Ayo's hand grips the edge of the desk; fist tightens.
9. **Scene 9 (13s):** Breaking the Loop. Ayo snaps the laptop shut. Loud physical snap; blue light dies instantly. The Beast recoils.
10. **Scene 10 (13s):** The Physical Stand. Ayo stands in the dark, bare feet touch cold floorboards, walks toward the shoes.
11. **Scene 11 (12s):** The Dawn Influx. Ayo opens the window blinds. Brilliant warm amber morning light floods the room.
12. **Scene 12 (10s):** Catharsis & Resolution. Ayo stands looking out into the dawn. The Beast is reduced to a harmless wisp. Whisper: *"Start."*
