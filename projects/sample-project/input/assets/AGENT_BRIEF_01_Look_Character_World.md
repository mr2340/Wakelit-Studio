# BRIEF 01 — Look, Character and World (Wakelit)

**Purpose:** teach you exactly what every image in a Wakelit film must look like, so that any scene you describe, generate or check is consistent and professional.
**Tags:** [MEASURED] measured on a reference video · [OBSERVED] seen but not measured · [OUR CHOICE] our own design · [UNKNOWN] not known, test or ask.
**Priority rule:** where a written description and an **approved image** disagree (Ayo's anchor image, the master room image), the **approved image wins**.

---
## 1. Screen format

### 1.1 The reference's frame [MEASURED]
- Video size 1280 × 720, 30 fps.
- The picture is **letterboxed**: the active picture is **1280 × 592** (about 2.16 : 1) with **64 px black bars** at the top and bottom.
- All captions, characters and props live inside the active picture.

### 1.2 Our frame [OUR CHOICE, scaled from the reference]
- Canvas **1920 × 1080, 30 fps**.
- Active picture **1920 × 888**, from **y = 96 to y = 984**. Black bars **96 px** top and bottom.
- **Safe composition rule:** every character, prop and caption must sit inside the active picture. When generating images or clips in 16:9, keep everything important inside the **central 82% of the height** (y = 96 to 984). Anything outside will be hidden by the bars.
- Thumbnails are the exception: they use the **full 16:9 frame**, no bars.

### 1.3 Stage style [OBSERVED]
The world is a **flat side-on stage**, like a theatre set seen from the front. There is no deep perspective. Characters stand on a **floor line** about **70–75% of the way down** the picture. Furniture is drawn as simple flat shapes with thin outlines.

## 3. Meet Ayo (explained so a child can understand it)

### 3.1 The big idea
Ayo is a **bean in a hoodie**. He is made of **simple, soft shapes**. He is not a realistic person. He has no nose, no ears, no neck, no fingers. His feelings show only through his **eyes** and **eyebrows**.

### 3.2 Build him from six shapes (draw him in this order)
1. **The body:** a soft bean (capsule). This is the mustard hoodie. It is a little wider than his head.
2. **The head:** a round, deep-brown ball sitting on top of the bean. It is merged into the body. **No neck.**
3. **The hood:** a soft ring or fold of mustard cloth at the bottom of the head, like a collar. The hood is always **down**, never over his head.
4. **The arms:** two thin noodle arms in mustard sleeves, hanging from the body. They end in **mitten hands**: round blobs with a tiny thumb bump. **No fingers.**
5. **The legs:** two short thin grey legs, coming out under the hoodie.
6. **The shoes:** two small white blobs.

### 3.3 Exact colours (approved anchor image)
| Part | Colour |
|---|---|
| Head | **#613729** (deep brown, flat, no texture) |
| Hoodie, sleeves and mitten hands | **#D2A23E** (mustard) |
| Legs | **#666668** (grey) |
| Shoes | **#EDEEF0** (near white) |
| Eyes | black dots |
| Outline | thin dark line around the character, about 2 px at 1080p, slightly wobbly like a hand drawing |

### 3.4 Proportions [OBSERVED on the approved anchor; the image wins]
- **Head** is about **one third of his total height**.
- **Body** (hoodie) is about **1.25 times as wide as the head**.
- **Legs** are short, about **a fifth of his height**.
- **Arms** are thin and long, and the hands end about **level with the bottom of the hoodie**.
- **Size on screen** [OBSERVED, from the reference's lead character, approximate]: in a wide standing shot he is about **half the picture height**. Seated, about **one third**. In a face close-up, his head fills about **60%** of the picture height.

### 3.5 The face (this is the most important part)
- **Eyes:** two small round black dots. Each has a **tiny white highlight** so the eyes look alive. Distance between the eyes is about **one third of the head width**.
- **Eyebrows:** two short, thick, curved lines above the eyes. **The brows carry the emotion.**
- **Mouth:** one small line or curve. It changes only a little.
- **Nothing else.** No nose. No ears. No cheeks. No nostrils. No hair.

### 3.6 The seven expressions (use these, don't invent new ones)
| Expression | Brows | Eyes | Mouth | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tired / worried** | inner ends raised, sloping down on the outside | half-lidded (a flat line cuts the top of each dot) | small flat line | the late-night spiral, guilt |
| **Blank / neutral** | straight and level | round and open | tiny straight line | calm, waiting, listening |
| **Determined** | lowered and angled inward (a firm "V") | focused, round | small firm line | the turning point, running |
| **Afraid / shocked** | raised high | **wider** (dots about 130% size) | tiny open "o" | the "365" realisation |
| **Relieved / soft smile** | gentle arch | soft, slightly lidded | small upward curve | morning, hope |
| **Sleeping** | none | closed, shown as two thin arcs | small relaxed line | the recovery nights |
| **Sad / low** | inner ends strongly raised | slightly watery, dots with a bigger highlight | tiny downward curve | loneliness, regret |

### 3.7 How Ayo moves (2D limited animation) [OBSERVED on the reference; the principle is general]
- **Breathing:** his body swells about 2% and settles every 2.5 seconds, always, even when "still".
- **Blinking:** every 2–4 seconds, quickly (3 frames).
- **Poses hold**, with small loops on top (head tilt, brow shift). He is not constantly flailing.
- **Walking:** simple walk cycle with leg alternation and a small body bob.
- **Big moves have anticipation:** a small squash before he stands, a small overshoot after.
- **Hands:** mitten hands tap, press, grip and hold as whole blobs. When he uses a phone, the mitten presses the glass with its whole side. Never draw individual fingers.
- **On a phone close-up** only his mustard mitten hands appear, at the left and right edges of the screen, like the reference.

### 3.8 Ayo never...
- gets a nose, ears, fingers, hair or a neck.
- changes hoodie colour, head colour, or proportions between shots.
- wears the hood up.
- looks realistic, 3D, shaded, glossy or anime.
- appears with the face turned fully away unless the script says so (back views are allowed from behind at the door).

### 3.9 The "Ayo test" (answer YES to all ten before approving any image)
1. Is the head deep brown and perfectly round? 2. Is the hoodie mustard, with the hood down? 3. Are there two clear black dot eyes with highlights? 4. Do the brows match the planned expression? 5. Is there no nose, no ears, no neck? 6. Are the hands round mittens with no fingers? 7. Are the legs grey and the shoes white? 8. Is the outline thin, dark and slightly wobbly? 9. Is the whole body inside the active picture? 10. Is the colouring flat, with no shading or gradient on the character?

## 4. The cast (friends and family must look like they belong in Ayo's world)

### 4.1 The family-resemblance rules [OUR CHOICE]
Everyone is built from the **same recipe as Ayo**:
- Same **bean body**, **round head merged into the body**, **no neck**.
- Same **two black dot eyes** (same size), **short brows**, **tiny mouth**. No noses or ears.
- Same **mitten hands**, **thin legs**, **small shoe blobs**, **thin dark outline**, **flat colour**.
- Each person is told apart by **three things only**: **head colour**, **hair or hat shape**, and **clothing colour**. A person may have **one small accessory**.
- **Ayo is the only person in mustard.** Nobody else wears mustard or amber clothing.
- Heights: friends and classmates are within 5% of Ayo's height. Adults are about 12% taller. Small children are about 20% shorter.
- Everyone's skin tone is chosen from the head-colour list below.

### 4.2 Head colours (flat, no shading)
Deep **#3B2418** · Dark **#4B2D1F** · Ayo **#613729** · Medium **#7A4A32** · Warm brown **#94603F** · Light brown **#B07A52**

### 4.3 Starting cast
| Name | Role | Head | Hair / hat | Clothing | One accessory |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ayo** | main character | #613729 | none | mustard hoodie #D2A23E, grey trousers | none |
| **Tobi** | best friend, always cheerful | #94603F | white backwards cap | teal tee **#3E8E8A** | none |
| **Emeka** | quiet friend | #3B2418 | short low-cut hair (a thin dark cap shape) | brick-red tee **#B5523B** | small round glasses |
| **Amaka** | classmate, kind | #7A4A32 | afro puff (round dark shape on top) | soft-pink hoodie **#D98E8E** | one small hair clip |
| **Mama** | Ayo's mother | #613729 | green head-wrap **#3E7A52** | purple blouse **#7A5AA8** | none |
| **Mr. Bello** | teacher / coach | #3B2418 | none | grey cardigan **#8A8F99** | a whistle on a cord |

Rules for new characters: make a new row, give them a unique head+hair+clothing combination that does not clash with anyone above, and get approval before using them.

### 4.4 Cast do-nots
- Do not copy the reference's characters (its cream lead, its beanie friends, its peach girl with brown hair). Our cast is ours.
- Do not give friends different eyes, noses or fingers.
- Do not let friends become more detailed than Ayo. **Ayo must remain the simplest and most recognisable figure on screen.**
- Never give a friend a dark-silhouette look; only the Shade is a silhouette.

### 4.5 Speech [OBSERVED]
- When a friend speaks on screen, use a **speech bubble**: a rounded **near-black box** with **white Comic-style text**, placed just above and beside the speaker's head, with a tiny tail. Text is one or two words ("hey!").
- The narrator's lines are **captions**, not bubbles (see Brief 02).

## 5. The Shade (the tempter, our own design)

### 5.1 What it is
A **small black blob** that lives under Ayo's bed. It does not shout or chase. It is **just there**. It feeds on "tomorrow". It is the voice of "later".

### 5.2 Design [OUR CHOICE]
- **Body:** a soft, rounded, solid-black blob with a slightly wavy edge, like thick ink. No limbs at the start. Later it may form two stubby shoulders (never hands with fingers).
- **Eyes:** two **thin horizontal slits** in pale blue **#9CC4FF**, softly glowing.
- **Rim light:** a **thin cold-blue outline glow** (about 3 px at 1080p) around its edge, brighter on the side facing the laptop. No other detail.
- **No mouth** ever. When it "smiles", the two slit eyes curve into **crescents**.
- It **never touches** Ayo. It only gets closer.

### 5.3 The Feeding Scale (its size is the story)
Sizes are a percentage of Ayo's **seated height**.
| Stage | Story moment | Size |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | peeks from under the bed | eyes only |
| 1 | slides out, "just there" | 40% (knee-high) |
| 2 | after the first "Mondays" | 60% (cat-sized in the story) |
| 3 | "never full" | 100% (as tall as the bed) |
| 4 | the peak, "The Beast" | fills the room, reaches the ceiling |
| 5 | NIGHT 1 (it came back) | 120% |
| 6 | NIGHT 2 | 60% |
| 7 | NIGHT 3 | 40% |
| 8 | NIGHT 30 | 25% (a coat on a chair, eyes closed) |
**Rule:** the Shade is bigger or smaller in every shot for a reason. Never change its size by accident.

### 5.4 Shade text
Its whisper captions are **pale blue #9CC4FF**, italic, in quotes, placed **beside the Shade at eye level** when it is visible, otherwise in the upper right.

## 6. The world

### 6.1 The bedroom (the home set) [OUR CHOICE; the master room image wins if different]
Seen **side-on**, left to right:
- **Desk** with a glowing laptop, an open notebook and a small lamp; a **wall clock** and a **wall calendar** above it.
- **Bed** (grey, simple), with a window above it showing faint night light.
- **Closed door** with a **thin amber strip of light** under it; **white running shoes** and a **guitar** stand beside the door.
- **Bedside table** with a **digital clock** in red digits **#D6453D**.
Every Wakelit film that uses the bedroom **uses the same layout**. If the camera is the same, the furniture is in the same place.

### 6.2 Other sets
- **Street at dusk/dawn:** a row of simple house shapes with lit windows, street lamps, a road line. **Warm windows (#E8A33D at low brightness)** against a dark street.
- **Corridor / lockers (reference device):** use only if the story needs a repeated location. Ours would be a **different place** (for example, a bus stop or a compound gate) so it is not copied.
- **Phone / laptop close-ups:** the screen fills the frame, background **blurred** dark room.

### 6.3 Light [OBSERVED]
- **Vignette:** edges darker than the centre in every scene (about 20–30%).
- **Glow:** light sources (lamp, screen, doorway, clock) have a **soft halo**. Nothing else glows.
- **Warm doorway:** a bright amber rectangle with a **radial warm glow** spreading from it across the floor and wall.
- **Grain:** very light film grain, about 4%, over everything.
- **Rim light on characters** appears only when a coloured light source is next to them (blue from the laptop, amber from the door).

### 6.4 Time devices [OBSERVED]
- **The clock:** a digital bedside clock with red digits that change to show time passing (for example 23:41 → 01:07). **The numbers must be consistent with the story.**
- **Counters:** "NIGHT 1 / 2 / 3 / 30" labels in the top left (see Brief 02 for animation).
- **Cards:** full black card with white text for time skips (see Brief 02).

## 7. Screens and UI

### 7.1 Phone [OBSERVED]
- Seen full-frame, portrait, held by **two mitten hands at the left and right edges**.
- Dark interface. Accent colour: **amber #E8A33D** (the reference used orange). Messages: **incoming bubbles slate grey #3A4150**, **outgoing bubbles amber**.
- Small dim-grey timestamps and day labels ("Thursday").
- The room behind the phone is **blurred and very dark**.

### 7.2 Laptop
- A **pale blue** screen, mostly blank, with a **blinking cursor**. The screen light is the **cold blue** that spills onto Ayo's face.

### 7.3 Notebook
- Cream paper **#EDE6D6**, faint ruled lines, handwriting in dark ink with a wobble.

### 7.4 Text inside generated images
AI often garbles words. If it does, **keep the shapes** (bubbles, lines, digits) and **add the words in the editor**. Never accept misspelled text in a final shot.

## 8. Consistency system (how every shot stays the same)

### 8.1 The lock kit (must exist before an episode starts)
| File | What |
|---|---|
| `ayo_anchor.png` | the approved Ayo front view |
| `ayo_tired.png`, `ayo_blank.png`, `ayo_determined.png`, `ayo_afraid.png`, `ayo_relieved.png`, `ayo_sleeping.png`, `ayo_sad.png` | the seven expressions |
| `shade_ref.png` | the approved Shade |
| `room_master.png` | the approved empty bedroom |
| `cast_sheet.png` | all friends side by side with Ayo (to check family resemblance and relative heights) |
| `palette.png` | swatches of every colour above |

### 8.2 Every generation uses these references
1. Attach **Ayo** (the right expression), the **room image**, and a **start frame**.
2. Begin the prompt with: *"Use the attached character Ayo exactly as drawn. Use the attached room as the set. Do not create any other character except where the prompt names one."*
3. End with: *"No new characters, no ghost characters, no big white eyes, no 3D, no shading, no text."*

### 8.3 Fixing drift
| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Ayo's face changes | regenerate with the closest expression image attached; if still wrong, **edit the last good frame** instead of generating from scratch |
| Hoodie colour shifts | add the hex values to the prompt: "mustard hoodie #D2A23E" |
| Room layout changes | attach `room_master.png` again; describe where each object is |
| Ayo looks realistic | add "flat 2D cartoon, simple bean-shaped body, no realism" and remove words like "young man", "skin", "fabric folds" |
| A new character appears | add "no other characters" and shorten the prompt |
| Still failing after three tries | **composite**: cut Ayo out as a transparent PNG and place him on an empty room image in the editor |

### 8.4 The approval ladder
Image **→** Ayo test (section 3.9) **→** palette check **→** room layout check **→** approved. Name approved files with `_APPROVED`.

## 9. Visual QC checklist (run on every shot)
- [ ] Inside the active picture (y 96–984)
- [ ] Ayo passes the Ayo test
- [ ] Colours match the palette; blue = temptation, amber = people
- [ ] Room layout matches the master room
- [ ] The Shade is the correct size for this story stage
- [ ] Vignette, glow and grain present
- [ ] Flat colour, no gradients on characters, no 3D
- [ ] No unwanted text, no misspelled text
- [ ] No copied reference characters or scenes
- [ ] Friends belong to the same family of shapes as Ayo
